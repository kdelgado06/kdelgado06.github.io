My Future Career: Working at Microsoft

Microsoft is a company Microsoft that started in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen. Microsoft is known for its software, hardware, and services globally. Such as Windows, Office, Azure, and Xbox. Over 1.4 billion people and businesses use Microsoft products and services. Microsoft has over 221,000 employees worldwide.
